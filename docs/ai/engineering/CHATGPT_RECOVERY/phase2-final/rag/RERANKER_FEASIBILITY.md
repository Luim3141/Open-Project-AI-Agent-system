## Why improvement is plausible; what may fail

Inference: jointly attending to query and passage can distinguish a requested SMS reminder from a generic task-status requirement better than independent BGE-M3 cosine. The official model is intended for query-passage reranking, making this a substantive change beyond lowering a cosine threshold. It could also reject the extra false positives introduced by complete passages. There is no QLDAPM accuracy/latency evidence for this model yet. Quantization, translated training data, technical identifiers, Vietnamese without accents,512-token truncation, copied PM/BA plans and missing source content can still defeat it. A relevance scorer may rank a plausible but unsupported document highly; abstention/citation gates and fresh hard negatives remain necessary.

The installed-assets inventory is therefore **not** a technical blocker. The concrete next step is a bounded isolated download/install and smoke benchmark with the pinned~136MB model/tokenizer payload, followed by a fresh benchmark if smoke succeeds. That step has not been executed in this metadata-only investigation.
